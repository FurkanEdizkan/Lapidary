# A26 — The sign-in drawing keeps moving

**Status:** ready
**Branch:** `feat/living-lattice`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web + docs · **Depends on:** A25 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "home page animation should be more dynamic and it should not stop after it's done one cycle".

## Decisions

- On sign-in only, after the gear draws itself it keeps turning about its axle, and a short Layout Blue mark travels
  around its tooth profile and its bore, the marking dye moving across the bench. The turn is one tooth pitch per
  cycle, which looks the same as a full turn and loops without a seam.
- It loops, so WCAG 2.2.2 applies: a visible Pause control stops it (and Play resumes it), and it stops while the tab
  is hidden. Under reduced motion it is drawn and still, as before.
- Everywhere else the lattice is unchanged: setup's intro draws once, and the still lattice on cards and stages never
  moves. DESIGN.md and CLAUDE.md say "nothing loops — except the sign-in drawing, which has a Pause control".

## Done when

Sign-in moves continuously after drawing, Pause stops it and Play resumes it, reduced motion is still, the gate
passes, and screenshots at 1440 and 390 show it well.

## Record

Headless Chrome on the built app (`vite preview`, lane 1's port), 1440×900 and 390×844: the gear draws in 2.4 s, then
turns one tooth pitch every 3 s with three Layout Blue marks travelling round the teeth, the hub's rim and the bore;
the lines' `d` change between frames; Pause holds them still and reads Play; at 390 the Pause sits inside the band.
Under reduced motion: drawn, still, no marks, no Pause. Tests: the turned gear keeps its frame and a pitch's turn lands
every line on a line (no seam); Pause/Play toggle; nothing but a live drawing offers a pause. Entry 85.80 kB gzip
(unchanged); the Lattice chunk carries anime.js (18.58 kB gzip, sign-in and setup only). Shots in
`/mnt/Storage2/lapidary-a26-shots`.
