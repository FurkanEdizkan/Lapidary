# A41 — First-run setup, filled in and driven end to end

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `feat/setup-whole`
**Wave:** 18 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** A40 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-21: a setup process for a first installation that makes the admin and configures the system, with
the steps sliding left and right. The wizard exists (A1, A5, A6, A20), but no browser flow drives it, its slide is
16px against DESIGN.md's 8px, the import-links choice can't be changed after setup, the sign-in pictures are not
offered, and how many imports run at once is an environment variable only. The owner chose to fill those gaps, and to
let an admin choose the import count.

## Done when

- An instance setting `import_workers` (1–8, default 2): chosen in the wizard's Options step and in Settings › Server,
  locked when `LAPIDARY_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` fixes it, applied by a restart. The worker default drops from 4 to 2 to
  match the 2 CPUs.
- The wizard's Options step also offers the sign-in page pictures.
- Settings offers the import-links switch to an admin.
- `Step` slides 8px each way.
- A rig flow `setup` drives the real wizard on a fresh stack, before `seed`.
- Gate and rig pass.
