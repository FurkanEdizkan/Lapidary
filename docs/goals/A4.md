## Done when

## Record

- **Sharing (`0066`).**
  - `part_share(part_id, user_id)` and `part.shared_with_everyone`.
  - `part_access` answers `Reader` for a part that is shared with everybody, or with this person. `auth::scope` lets a
    reader `GET`/`HEAD` it and answers anything else with `403` and a sentence.
  - Blob reachability counts shared parts. `delete_parts` deletes a part's shares, and the table-guard test lists
    `part_share`.
  - `GET|PUT /api/parts/{id}/shares` is owner only: the guard refuses a reader's `PUT`, and the handler refuses a
    reader's `GET`.
  - `GET /api/shared` lists other people's parts shared with you, newest first. `PartDetail.yours` tells the page.
- **Accounts (`people.rs`).**
  - `GET /api/people` lists everybody on the install, which is who you can share with.
  - Admin only:
    - `POST /api/people` makes an account, with an empty "My models" library;
    - `PUT /api/people/{id}/password` signs that person out everywhere;
    - `DELETE /api/people/{id}`, never yourself, gives that person's libraries and parts to the admin. A name the admin
      already uses gets ` (<username>)`, and shares with the admin of what is now theirs go.
  - `PUT /api/profile/password` needs the current password and signs out the person's other sessions.
- **Web.**
  - `ShareDialog`: everybody, or chosen people. It is on the part page's ⋯ menu and in the grid selection bar (one `PUT`
    per part).
  - Shared with me is a place in the header (`/shared`, a gallery grid with a skeleton while it loads).
  - People (`/people`): the list for everyone, and add / new password / remove for an admin, linked from the account menu
    for admins only.
  - Profile has a password change.
  - On a shared model's page the actions are replaced by "Shared with you…", and `recordable={yours}` turns off the tag,
    source, field and picture editors.
  - `session.currentUser()` is what the gate read.
