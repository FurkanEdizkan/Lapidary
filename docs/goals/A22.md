# A22 — Dialogs with presence

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `feat/dialog-presence`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A18 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

The redesign (REDESIGN.md, owner 2026-10-18): dialogs arrive and leave with Motion's presence instead of CSS
keyframes that cannot play an exit, and their insides follow the new direction.

## Decisions

- `Dialog.tsx` animates its scrim and panel through `Presence` and the `motion-react.tsx` primitives: in over 180 ms
  (opacity, 8px rise, scale from 0.98), out over 120 ms. Focus trap, Escape, focus return and `stopPropagation` on
  inner forms (A9) are unchanged. `panel-in` and `scrim-in` leave `styles.css` once nothing uses them.
- ImportDialog, ShareDialog and FolderPicker keep every accessible name the rig clicks; their layout and spacing
  follow DESIGN.md.

## Done when

Dialogs open and close with presence, still under reduced motion; the rig's dialog flows and the gate pass.

## Record

**Built.**
- `Dialog.tsx` draws its scrim through `Fade` and its box through a new `Panel` primitive in `motion-react.tsx`
  (opacity, y 8→0, scale 0.98→1 over `--duration-base`; out over `--duration-fast`, `transition-none` like the rest).
  A caller gets the exit by mounting the dialog inside `Presence` (`<Presence>{open ? <X key=… /> : null}</Presence>`);
  the API, focus trap, Escape, focus return, sizes and portal are unchanged, and a dialog outside a `Presence` still
  just vanishes. While a box leaves it has no `role`/`aria-modal`, is `aria-hidden` and `inert`, its scrim lets
  clicks through, Escape and the trap are off, and focus has already gone back (the focus effect is keyed on
  `usePresent()`), so the 120 ms exit holds nobody up and the rig's `[role="dialog"]` selectors see it gone at once.
- `mechanical()` returns duration 0 under reduced motion. A primitive's own `transition` outranks `MotionRoot`'s, so
  `Fade`'s exit (and every other explicit `mechanical('fast')`) still faded for 120 ms with reduced motion on; now
  everything jumps, as the doctrine says. `MotionRoot` simply passes `mechanical('base')`.
- Wrapped in `Presence`: Import, link import, share (grid and part page), delete (grid, part page, people), move and
  pick-category (grid, part page), the folder picker (setup, settings), new library, free render cache, fields,
  densities, and the category tree's duplicate, new, rename and delete dialogs.

  safe-area padding, `max-h-[92vh]`); the title is Scope (15px/600, Quicklime); leads are `Page.ts`'s `LEAD` (Ash,
  70ch); sections are `SECTION_TITLE` over a scribe line (`DIALOG_SECTION`); one standing verb at the foot, right

  ("Add 3 selected", Use this folder, Import, Share, Delete); `overscroll-contain` on every scroller. Import's picture
