# A22 — Dialogs with presence

**Status:** claimed
**Branch:** `feat/dialog-presence`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A18 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

The redesign (REDESIGN.md, owner 2026-10-18): dialogs arrive and leave with Motion's presence instead of CSS
keyframes that cannot play an exit, and their insides follow the new direction.

## Decisions

- `Dialog.tsx` animates its scrim and panel through `Presence` and the `motion-react.tsx` primitives: in over 180 ms
  (opacity, 8px rise, scale from 0.98), out over 120 ms. Focus trap, Escape, focus return and `stopPropagation` on
  inner forms (A9) are unchanged. `panel-in` and `scrim-in` leave `styles.css` once nothing uses them.
- ImportDialog, ShareDialog and FolderPicker keep every accessible name the rig clicks; their layout and spacing
  follow DESIGN.md.

## Done when

Dialogs open and close with presence, still under reduced motion; the rig's dialog flows and the gate pass.
