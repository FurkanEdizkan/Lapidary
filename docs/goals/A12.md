# A12 — Grid, size, measure and axes in the 3D view

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `feat/viewer-tools`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A11 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "Can we add grid and size measurement like tools and 3d view coordinates like info for viewers as
well". This brings back a measuring tool the pivot (2026-10-03) took out of the viewer, in its mesh-only form: no
kernel, no entities to snap to, and every figure approximate and labelled so.

## Decisions

- In the view, three toggles: **Grid** (a floor grid under the part in millimetres, its spacing 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ for
  the part's size, and said), **Size** (the box around the part and its ≈ width × depth × height), **Measure** (two
  clicks on the model, a line between them, ≈ distance and ≈ ΔX ΔY ΔZ, and each point's ≈ coordinates).
- An axis gizmo in the corner (three's `ViewHelper`): X, Y and Z as the file has them, and a click turns the view to
  look along one. Instantly under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Every number through `Figure`, with its ≈. A click is a pick only when the pointer did not move: a drag still turns
  the view. No hover readout: on a model of millions of triangles a pick is a scan of all of them.

## Done when

The three tools and the gizmo work on the owner's files in the stage and on the part's page; the gate and the rig
pass.
