# A12 — Grid, size, measure and axes in the 3D view

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `feat/viewer-tools`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A11 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "Can we add grid and size measurement like tools and 3d view coordinates like info for viewers as
well". This brings back a measuring tool the pivot (2026-10-03) took out of the viewer, in its mesh-only form: no
kernel, no entities to snap to, and every figure approximate and labelled so.

## Decisions

- In the view, three toggles: **Grid** (a floor grid under the part in millimetres, its spacing 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ for
  the part's size, and said), **Size** (the box around the part and its ≈ width × depth × height), **Measure** (two
  clicks on the model, a line between them, ≈ distance and ≈ ΔX ΔY ΔZ, and each point's ≈ coordinates).
- An axis gizmo in the corner (three's `ViewHelper`): X, Y and Z as the file has them, and a click turns the view to
  look along one. Instantly under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Every number through `Figure`, with its ≈. A click is a pick only when the pointer did not move: a drag still turns
  the view. No hover readout: on a model of millions of triangles a pick is a scan of all of them.

## Done when

The three tools and the gizmo work on the owner's files in the stage and on the part's page; the gate and the rig
pass.

## Record

- `Viewer.tsx`: a toolbar over the view (Grid, Size, Measure, `aria-pressed`) and a readout in its lower left. The
  grid is three's `GridHelper` laid on the part's floor (XY, Z up as the file has it) at `gridStep` — 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ
  for about ten squares across — and its step is said without ≈, because it is the step it was drawn at. Size is
  `Box3Helper` and the box's ≈ extents; Measure raycasts a click (a pointer that moved under 4 px) against the drawn
  mesh, marks the point, joins two with a line, and says each point's ≈ coordinates, the ≈ distance and ≈ ΔX ΔY ΔZ;
  a third click starts over. Every measured figure goes through `Figure`. `ViewHelper` in the corner, labelled X Y Z,
  turns the view on a click, instantly under reduced motion. The view clears once a frame (`autoClear` off): the
  gizmo's own render had wiped the part. A kept picture leaves the aids out.
- `viewer-math.ts`: `gridStep`, `millimetres`, `between`, tested.
- The viewer's comment no longer says "no measuring": the pivot's measuring snapped to CAD entities; this one is on
  the file's own triangles and says ≈.
- Checks: vitest 448. Rig: `view-tools` — size, grid and two clicks on the flange in headless Chrome, the view's size
  held to the part's stated box — and the screenshot shows the part, grid, box, line and gizmo. A run of only
  `view-original` and `view-tools` failed `view-original` on a cold first page (the button not yet rendered); the full
  rig passes it.
- **Left.** No hover readout and no snapping: a pick scans every triangle (a fraction of a second on millions). The
