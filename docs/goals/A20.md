# A20 — Setup: intro, stepper wizard, done moment

**Status:** ready
**Branch:** `feat/setup-stepper`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A19 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "for setup before setup small animation transition to setup then setup page". The brief is
REDESIGN.md (§ The intro into setup, § The setup wizard); the written rules are DESIGN.md § Doorway and § Stepper.

## Decisions

- The intro plays once per page load inside `--duration-intro`: the lattice draws in at the centre, then travels to
  the Doorway's left column (`layoutId`) as the wizard rises. A key, a click or Skip ends it; reduced motion skips it.
- The wizard is the Doorway split: lattice and a vertical stepper (Account, Server, Options; `aria-current="step"`;
  a grey rail marker on the layout spring) left, the step right in `Presence` with `Step` (direction-aware). One
  `<form>`; nothing is saved until Finish; a refused setup code returns to its step.
- Done: the lattice settles and the diamond fills while the port move or restart is followed, never delaying it.
- An already set-up install's `/setup` is no dead end: it says so and offers sign-in as a standing action.

## Done when

The intro, every step, Back, a refused code and Done look right at 1440 and 390 with reduced motion on and off;
`setup.test.tsx` covers the stepper, Skip and reduced motion; the gate passes; the entry stays within budget.

## Record

**Built.**
- **The intro** (`routes/setup.tsx`): once per page load (a module flag, set when it ends), only when motion is
  allowed. The Lattice draws at the centre with the diamond under it for `--duration-scene`, then the same element
  moves into the Doorway's left column (`Indicator id="setup-lattice"`, the layout spring) while the heading, the
  form and the stepper `Rise`. Lines keep drawing through the travel and are settled by `--duration-intro` (900 ms,
  Lattice's new `total` prop). The clock starts when the lazy Lattice chunk resolves, so a cold load never spends
  the intro on an empty screen. Any key, any pointer press or **Skip** ends it at its end state (no travel, the
  drawing stilled, no accent left). `<title>` renders throughout.
- **One element, no remount**: the lattice's wrapper switches between `fixed inset-0` (centre) and `contents`
  (column), so the drawing never restarts. That needed Doorway's band to stop centring with a translate (a
  transformed ancestor captures `position: fixed`); it centres by flex now, no visible change on sign-in.
- **The stepper** (DESIGN.md § Stepper): at `lg` an `<ol role="list" aria-label="Setup steps">` under the lattice,
  `aria-current="step"` on the current step (Quicklime), done steps in Ash with Phosphor's `check` (vendored in
  `Icon.tsx`) and an sr-only "done", steps ahead in Graphite; a 1px Scribe Line rail with a 2px Edge marker
  (`Indicator id="setup-rail"`). Below `lg`: "Step 2 of 3 · Server" and a hairline whose Edge part is the progress.
- **Steps** in `Presence mode="wait"` with `Step` direction-aware (Back reverses). Still one `<form>`, nothing saved
  until Finish, Back keeps what was typed. At `lg` the column has the tallest step's height, so the heading does
  not jump between steps.
- **Refusals**: `Refused` now carries the server's `code`. `wrong_code` goes back to step 1 with the sentence under
  the setup code field (`aria-invalid`, focused); any other refusal shows at the foot of step 1.
- **Done**: from Finish until the page leaves, the mark's diamond fills (an accent square fading and scaling in,
  CSS, 280 ms), the lattice is still, and `<p role="status">Setting up…</p>` stays. Nothing awaits it: the
  existing `followRestart`/`followPort`/`assign` chain is unchanged.
- **Already set up**: "Lapidary is set up", one sentence, and **Sign in** as the standing control, beside the still
  lattice.
- A22's `Presence` around the folder picker is kept as A22 left it.

**Decided without the owner.**
- After the intro, focus stays where the browser leaves it rather than moving to the heading: a programmatic focus
  after a key press drew the blue ring at rest (`:focus-visible` is unlayered and outranks `outline-none`).
- The rail marker is Edge on the lamp, 2.81:1. It is supplementary (the step's colour, the check and
  `aria-current` carry the state), so no new contrast pair was added; every text pairing used here is already in
  `contrast.test.ts` (all four text tokens on the lamp).
- A stilled Lattice renders its lines on fresh nodes (`key` on the group): reverting anime's drawable after a
  drawing left the strokes blank, which Skip and Done both hit in a real browser (jsdom cannot see it, as it has
  no `getTotalLength`).
