# A28 — The library wall: sign-in shows the library's own pictures

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `feat/the-wall`
**Wave:** 14 · **Kind:** api + db + web + docs · **Depends on:** A27 · **Migrations:** `0069`

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18, shown ten concepts for the sign-in drawing after A27's brilliant ("I don't like the mesh design or
diamond"), chose **the library wall**: columns of cards scrolling past, "empty initial then we can populate them with
the items on our real library, by default it is all and random but we can select items to not be present on this home
view". Asked who may appear on a page anyone can open, they chose: parts **shared with everyone** only; a switch on
each part plus one for the whole wall in Settings; **pictures only**.

## Decisions

- This reverses DESIGN.md's "the left half shows no data, ever" for one thing: pictures. The wall shows the first
  picture of parts that are shared with everyone and not hidden from it, in random order, and nothing else — no name,
  id, count or figure. A private part, or one shared only with chosen people, never appears.
- `part.on_wall` (`0069`, default true) is the per-part switch, set by the owner with `PUT /api/parts/{id}/wall`, on
  the part page. `instance_setting` `sign_in_wall` (missing is on) is the admin's switch for the whole wall, in Settings.
- Two routes a browser calls signed out: `GET /api/wall` (up to 48 pictures, `no-store`) and `GET /api/wall/{hash}`
  for a picture over the inline line, which re-checks the whole condition and is the same 404 as an unknown hash
  otherwise.
- With nothing to show — a fresh install, nothing shared, the wall switched off — the wall is its empty frames, still
  moving. A few pictures repeat to fill the columns. Pause, the hidden-tab stop and reduced motion as in A26.
- Setup, the placeholders and the dashboard keep A27's still drawing; nothing exists to show during setup.

## Done when

A part shared with everyone shows on the signed-out sign-in page; hiding it, unsharing it or switching the wall off
takes it off both routes; a non-owner cannot set the switch; reduced motion is a still wall; the rig and gate pass;
screenshots at 1440 and 390.
