# A34 — Counts that do not slow down with the library

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `perf/counts`
**Wave:** 16 · **Kind:** db + api + web · **Depends on:** A33 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Scale report item 2 (and item 8's dashboard timing). At 20k parts facets take 84–98 ms and the category tree 86 ms and
1.36 MB, and while a scan runs the grid asks for facets and storage again about once a second.

## Decisions

- During a scan the grid refreshes its counts at most every 5 s, and once when the scan ends.
- The facet groups come from one query rather than one per group.
- The category tree sends each folder's own name and its parent, not its whole path.
- The dashboard's widgets are timed at 20k.

## Done when

Facets under 40 ms and the tree under 400 kB at 20k; at most one facet request per 5 s during a scan; gate and rig pass.
