# P2 — Browse by creator

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `feat/creator-browse`
**Wave:** 4 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** W0 (and P1, if releases have landed — a release names a creator) · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Every archive-style library is navigated by who made the thing, and we already record it: `part_source` holds `vendor`,
`url`, `external_id`, `title`, price and licence, and `part_provenance` holds which installation a pulled part came
from. None of it is navigable — a person can read a vendor on one part's page and has no way to ask for the rest.

## Facts to check before building

- `part_source`: `vendor` is free text and a part may carry **several** sources (`0015_part_images_and_sources.sql`), so
  "the creator of a part" is not a single value. The list is distinct vendors, grouped case-insensitively.
- The facet query in `crates/lapidary-db/src/repo.rs` (formats, materials, tags, fields) — its exact-count threshold
  (`EXACT_FACET_ROWS`), how it narrows by the other filters but never by itself, and what a fourth facet costs when its
  value lives on a joined table rather than on `part`.
- The grid's filter parameters (`GridQuery`) and how a new one reaches the URL, the saved filters and the bundle plan.
- `PartCard` is at sqlx's 16-column `FromRow` ceiling (W0's note): a vendor on the card may cost a struct change, or the
  card may not carry it at all.

## Rules

- **A creator is a string, not a table.** A creator table needs merging, renaming and somebody to rule on duplicates,
  and nothing asks for that yet. Grouping is `lower(trim(vendor))`, with the commonest spelling shown.
- The creators list is a read of what parts say; it never writes a creator anywhere.
- One definition of the filter, in `GridQuery`, used by the grid, the facet counts and the creator page alike.

## Stages

1. **Reads** — a library's creators (name, part count, a few thumbnails) and the grid filtered by creator, as a
   `GridQuery` field beside format, material and tag.
   **Tests:** two spellings of one vendor are one row; a part with two sources counts once per creator, not twice; the
   filter narrows the grid and the facet counts agree with the list.
2. **Facet** — creator joins format, material and tag in `GET /api/libraries/{id}/facets`, narrowed by the others and
   never by itself, and past the exact-count threshold it behaves as the others do.
3. **Web** — `/creators` (the index, by count and by name) and a creator page that is the grid pre-filtered; the vendor
   on the part page becomes the link there; the `creators` strings block.
   **Tests:** the index renders with `fetch` mocked; a creator with one part reads in the singular; the page's title is
   the creator's name.

## Done when

Every part with a vendor is reachable from `/creators` in two clicks, the facet counts match the index, `verify slice` is
green, and the Record names how many creators the seeded corpus produced.
