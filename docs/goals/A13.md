# A13 — Packs: archives in the library, kept packed

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `feat/packs`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** A12 · **Migrations:** 0067

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: a folder such as `Creators/Bite The Bullet/Bite The Bullet - Bleeding Procession` holds a creator's
releases as archives (13 zip, 1 rar, 4.4 GB). It should be addable, described, and downloadable whenever wanted,
without being unpacked or drawn. The owner chose: each archive is one item, filed under its folder's category; never
unpacked; zip and 7z list what is inside.

## Decisions

- A **pack** is a `.zip`, `.7z` or `.rar` in the models folder, read where it is like a model: a scan and the Import
  browser take it, hash first (streamed, never read whole), and it becomes a part with no mesh figures and no 3D view.
  Its download is the archive, byte for byte. Nothing is extracted or written.
- Its contents — names and sizes from the archive's index, at most 5,000 — are read once at ingest and kept on the
  revision (`0067`, `revision.contents`), because opening a part never reads its file. A RAR, an encrypted index or
  an unreadable one is still a pack, with a sentence instead of a list.
- A pack unchanged since it was indexed (same size and modified time) is not hashed again by a rescan.
- Archives under the models folder's `uploads/` are not packs: those are uploads A10 already unpacked into parts.
- An uploaded RAR becomes a pack instead of being refused; an uploaded zip or 7z is still unpacked (A10).

## Done when

The owner's folder becomes 14 packs under its category, zips listing their contents and the RAR saying why not; the
downloads match the originals byte for byte; nothing offers View or export; the gate and the rig pass.
