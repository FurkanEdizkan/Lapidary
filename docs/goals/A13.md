# A13 — Packs: archives in the library, kept packed

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `feat/packs`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** A12 · **Migrations:** 0067

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: a folder such as `Creators/Bite The Bullet/Bite The Bullet - Bleeding Procession` holds a creator's
releases as archives (13 zip, 1 rar, 4.4 GB). It should be addable, described, and downloadable whenever wanted,
without being unpacked or drawn. The owner chose: each archive is one item, filed under its folder's category; never
unpacked; zip and 7z list what is inside.

## Decisions

- A **pack** is a `.zip`, `.7z` or `.rar` in the models folder, read where it is like a model: a scan and the Import
  browser take it, hash first (streamed, never read whole), and it becomes a part with no mesh figures and no 3D view.
  Its download is the archive, byte for byte. Nothing is extracted or written.
- Its contents — names and sizes from the archive's index, at most 5,000 — are read once at ingest and kept on the
  revision (`0067`, `revision.contents`), because opening a part never reads its file. A RAR, an encrypted index or
  an unreadable one is still a pack, with a sentence instead of a list.
- A pack unchanged since it was indexed (same size and modified time) is not hashed again by a rescan.
- Archives under the models folder's `uploads/` are not packs: those are uploads A10 already unpacked into parts.
- An uploaded RAR becomes a pack instead of being refused; an uploaded zip or 7z is still unpacked (A10).

## Done when

The owner's folder becomes 14 packs under its category, zips listing their contents and the RAR saying why not; the
downloads match the originals byte for byte; nothing offers View or export; the gate and the rig pass.

## Record

- `lapidary-core`: `PACK_EXTENSIONS` (zip, 7z, rar) and `is_pack_file`, beside `is_model_file` and not inside it; the
  scan and the Import browser take packs, nothing else changed meaning.
- `0067_pack_contents.sql`: `revision.contents jsonb`. `IngestRequest`/`RevisionRequest`/`RevisionWrite` take
  `measurements: Option<_>`; a pack writes NULL figures and NULL provenance. `PgRevisions::set_contents`, after commit,
  as the centre of mass. `CurrentRevision` carries the file's size and modified time.
- `handler.rs`: `index` is a wrapper over `index_body(Body::Mesh | Body::Pack)`; `ingest_pack` stats, skips a pack
  whose size and modified time match the current revision's, else hashes it with `update_reader` (streamed) and reads
  its contents with `archive::contents` (zip central directory raw, 7z header; RAR, an encrypted or broken index →
  `unread`). An uploaded RAR goes the same way from the inbox. `scan.rs` drops packs under `uploads/`.
- API: `PartDetail.pack: PackContents | null`; `PartSummary.approximate` is false for a part with no figures.
- Web: Geometry's place on a pack's page is "Inside this pack" (the release's own top folder said once); no View and
  no 3MF export for a pack; a pack's card reads "Pack · 459.7 MB" and no "approximate".
- CLAUDE.md's "Meshes in, meshes out" names packs.
- Checks: unit (contents for zip, 7z, RAR, broken, missing), handler (a scan of a zip, a RAR and an `uploads/` zip:
  two packs, no figures, contents, the RAR's sentence, a rescan skipped; an uploaded RAR kept as a pack), vitest (the
  pack section, the shared folder, no view, no export). Live, the owner's "Bleeding Procession" hard-linked into a

  archives; added and scanned, 14 packs in 3.5 s under one category; Stigmatic Nuns lists 188 files; the RAR says why
