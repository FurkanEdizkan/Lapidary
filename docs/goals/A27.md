# A27 — The cut: the sign-in drawing becomes a gem

**Status:** claimed
**Branch:** `feat/the-cut`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web + docs · **Depends on:** A26 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18, of A26's turning gear: "I want something unique something nice not just simple gear". Of three
concepts they chose **the cut**, and chose that the still placeholders match it.

## Decisions

- The Lattice's part is a round brilliant, the stone a lapidary cuts and the shape the brand's diamond already names,
  drawn as a faceted wireframe in a three-quarter view with hidden lines: edges of facets turned away are faint.
- On sign-in a rough stone draws itself, is cut facet by facet into the brilliant (each facet catching Layout Blue as
  it is cut), and then for ever turns one eighth (its symmetry, so the loop has no seam), settles and holds, while a
  glint runs across the facets that face the light. The Pause, the hidden-tab stop and reduced motion are as in A26.
- Everywhere the still lattice stands in for a missing picture it is now the brilliant's settled frame, grey, never
  moving. Setup draws the brilliant, once.

## Done when

Sign-in draws, cuts and keeps turning; Pause holds it; reduced motion shows the still brilliant with no blue; cards,
the stage and the dashboard show the still brilliant sized to fit; the gate passes; screenshots at 1440 and 390.
