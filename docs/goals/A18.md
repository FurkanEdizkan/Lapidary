# A18 — Motion foundation: `motion` pinned beside anime.js

**Status:** ready
**Branch:** `feat/motion-foundation`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A17 (doctrine), started beside it · **Migrations:** —

## Why

The redesign (REDESIGN.md, owner 2026-10-18) uses Motion (motion.dev's MIT `motion` package) for presence, layout
and shared-element animation, beside anime.js for timelines and SVG drawing. This goal adds the engine and the one
place it is imported, without changing what any page looks like.

## Decisions

- `motion` pinned exactly at 13.5.1: the newest release at least two weeks old on 2026-10-18 (14.0.0 shipped the same
  day as a new major). Bundled by Vite; nothing is fetched at run time.
- `animejs` stays imported only by `lib/motion.ts`; `motion/react` only by `lib/motion-react.tsx` and
  `lib/motion-features.ts`; `framer-motion` (its dependency) never directly. A test holds all three.
- The anime-free token reading moves to `lib/motion-tokens.ts`, so the root's `MotionRoot` never pulls anime into the
  entry chunk. `domMax` loads lazily through `LazyMotion`.
- `MotionRoot` honours reduced motion by giving every transition zero duration, not only by dropping transforms.
- Every primitive carries `transition-none`: `styles.css` gives every element a base transition, which would smear
  Motion's per-frame writes.

## Done when

The engine is in, pages look the same, the containment test and the gate pass, and the entry chunk's growth is
recorded below.

## Record

`vite build`, gzip, before → after:

| chunk | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| entry (`index-*.js`) | 83.90 kB | 94.16 kB (+10.3) |
| anime.js (`motion-*.js`) | 13.85 kB | merged by Rollup into the shared animation chunk below |
| Motion features + anime (`motion-features-*.js`, lazy) | — | 29.78 kB |
| `Viewer-*.js` (lazy) | 155.76 kB | 155.75 kB |

The entry's growth is `LazyMotion` and `MotionConfig` themselves; `domMax` loads on first animation. That is 2.3 kB
over the 8 kB REDESIGN.md guessed. The owner chose Motion knowing it costs bytes; the budget for A19–A25 is now the
entry at most **4 kB** past 94.16 kB, and anything that animates only at the front door loads lazily.
