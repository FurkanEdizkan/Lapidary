# R6 — The rig, and the documents

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `docs/visual-library`
**Wave:** 5 · **Kind:** tooling + lead · **Depends on:** R1, R2, R3 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

The rig asserts a product that no longer exists, and the documents describe one. Of the two, **the documents
matter more**: `CLAUDE.md` is the constitution every future session reads first, and three of its
non-negotiable rules are now false.

## The rig

**Dead:** `thumbnails` (asserts every card carries a `data:` render), `duplicates`, `section-explode-pmi`
(the only flow that proved the real kernel ran), `reduced-motion-no-spin`, `hover-heap` (50 hovers for WebGL
contexts that no longer exist), and `exit2` with `scripts/e2e/exit2.py`.

**Replaced:**
- **`pictures`** — every card shows a photo or its format placeholder, and **nothing fetches per card**. The
  one-request property is the half of the old flow worth keeping.
- **`view-original`** — press view, assert a first frame, and assert the network fetched
  `download?variant=original` and nothing GLB-shaped.
- `part-detail` repoints from a STEP fixture to a mesh; `measure` loses its "L2 has to arrive" settle and
  will need its pick coordinates re-found, because L2 and the original are not the same triangles.

**New seed assertions in `stack.sh`:** the six `step/` fixtures are **refused by name** and the part count
drops accordingly; `derivative` holds no render kinds; and — the one that catches every loop at once —
**restart the worker and assert the `job` table gained nothing.** The old thumbnail wait loop goes.

## The documents

`CLAUDE.md`'s product rules, three of which are now false:

| Rule | Why it is false | What it becomes |
|---|---|---|
| "We visualize, **measure**, route, version" | nothing measures a B-rep any more; mesh facts are figures, not measurement | catalogue, picture, search, route, version |
| "Format negotiation is automatic … **CAD gets STEP**" | STEP is refused at ingest | meshes in, mesh or 3MF out |
| "The open path never touches a source file" | pressing **view** now hands the browser the source file deliberately | the open path reads metadata and pictures; the viewer is asked for, and then it reads the file |

Also: "Measurement must not lie" **stays** and still binds — a figure from a mesh is approximate and says so.

Then `PRODUCT.md`; `FEATURES.md` §§2–4 (the viewer, measurement, assembly and similarity rows, and the stale
"17th column" line at :334); `DATA.md` §1.5 and §3.8; `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s layout, viewer and kernel rows; and
a **ROADMAP ledger entry** recording that Phases 3, 4 and 6's geometry halves were built, measured, and then
deliberately removed — with the sha where each left, so the history stays legible rather than looking like
work that never happened.

## Re-scope on the board

- **P1** (release archives) loses its dependency on shape profiles.
- **P4** (explore) loses the near-duplicate line.
- **P2** (browse by creator) is unaffected and becomes more central: with no renders, the creator and the
  picture are most of what a card is.

## Done when

`verify slice` green, the rig passes on a stack built from `main`, and no document claims a feature this
product no longer has.
