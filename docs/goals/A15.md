# A15 — A page's description and categories, pictures first, and a gallery in the owner's order

**Status:** claimed
**Branch:** `feat/picture-order`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** A14 · **Migrations:** 0068

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18, after Fetch details worked on MyMiniFactory (A14): use the page's description and categories too;
the dashboard's pictures should be bigger; when a part has a real picture it should be the one shown, not the
model's snapshot; and the order of a part's pictures should be the owner's, by drag and drop.

## Decisions

- Fetch details keeps the page's **description** on the part's source row (`0068`, `part_source.description`) and shows
  it under Source on the part's page. A page fetched again refreshes its row rather than skipping it.
- A site's **categories** (MyMiniFactory gives them) join the part's tags: the library's own categories stay the
  owner's grouping. Page tags and categories are added to the tags a part already has, never replacing one.
- A **picture of the thing** (uploaded, from an address, from a page) is placed after the other pictures of the thing
  and **before** any picture of the model (a 3D-view snapshot, a 3MF's own thumbnail), so a card shows the
  photograph. Nothing already placed moves except model pictures stepping back one.
- The gallery is reordered by **drag and drop** on the part's page, with move-earlier/move-later buttons for a
  keyboard; `PUT /api/parts/{id}/images/order` takes every picture id in the new order and refuses any other set.
  The first is the card's picture.
- The dashboard's part lists show their pictures at 56px instead of 32px.

## Done when

On the Lieutenant, the MyMiniFactory pictures come before the snapshot after reordering, the card shows the first,
the description shows under Source and the categories are tags; a new page picture lands ahead of a snapshot; the
gate and the rig pass.
