# A32 — Ingest within its memory, four at a time

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `perf/ingest-budget`
**Wave:** 15 · **Kind:** ingest + deploy · **Depends on:** A31 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18, "then 3 and 4". The first scan of the owner's 309 GB corpus (3,286 parts) took 888 s at 3.7 files a
second with two workers, and the app peaked at 2.83 GiB of its 3 GiB limit: a few large 3MF kits parsing together come
close to the OOM killer.

## Decisions

- **One parse budget for the process**, in MiB of estimated peak memory: a mesh takes `size × factor` of it before it
  is read (STL 2, OBJ 3, 3MF 6 — a 3MF is a zip of XML that inflates as it parses), and gives it back when it is filed.
  A file bigger than the whole budget takes all of it and runs alone. Packs are streamed and take none.
- With that in place the worker runs **four at a time** by default (was two), so small files keep flowing while a
  large one parses. `LAPIDARY_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` still sets it.
- The container's CPU and memory ceilings are unchanged; they are the owner's to raise.

## Done when

A fresh scan of the corpus peaks below the 3 GiB limit with room to spare, and is no slower; gate and rig pass.
