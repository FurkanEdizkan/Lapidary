# A32 — Ingest within its memory, four at a time

**Status:** open
**Branch:** `perf/ingest-budget`
**Wave:** 15 · **Kind:** ingest + deploy · **Depends on:** A31 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18, "then 3 and 4". The first scan of the owner's 309 GB corpus (3,286 parts) took 888 s at 3.7 files a
second with two workers, and the app peaked at 2.83 GiB of its 3 GiB limit: a few large 3MF kits parsing together come
close to the OOM killer.

## Decisions

- **One parse budget for the process**, in MiB of estimated peak memory: a mesh takes `size × factor` of it before it
  is read (STL 2, OBJ 3, 3MF 6 — a 3MF is a zip of XML that inflates as it parses), and gives it back when it is filed.
  A file bigger than the whole budget takes all of it and runs alone. Packs are streamed and take none.
- With that in place the worker runs **four at a time** by default (was two), so small files keep flowing while a
  large one parses. `LAPIDARY_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` still sets it.
- The container's CPU and memory ceilings are unchanged; they are the owner's to raise.

## Done when

A fresh scan of the corpus peaks below the 3 GiB limit with room to spare, and is no slower; gate and rig pass.

## Notes so far (2026-10-19, paused by the owner; not merged)

What is on the branch: a process-wide parse budget (`PARSE_BUDGET`, 1536 MiB; `parse_cost_mib` = size × 6 for 3MF,
3 OBJ, 2 STL) taken in `ingest_one` before a mesh is read, and the compose default concurrency 2 → 4. Unit test
`a_meshs_parse_cost_follows_its_format_and_never_exceeds_the_budget`. Gate not yet run on the last commit; rig not run.

What the measurements say — **the budget as written does not bound memory, and 4 workers must not merge yet**:
- `docker stats` / `memory.peak` overstate it: they include file cache (2.4 GB of the 3 GB was reclaimable
  `inactive_file` after the corpus scan). The real number is `anon` in the container's cgroup `memory.stat`; the
  sampler is `/home/jbo/.claude/jobs/27942f58/tmp/scale/anon.sh`.
- The big set `/mnt/Storage2/lapidary-check-bigset` (73 hard links: the 41 STL and 5 3MF over 100 MB, the 27 3MF of
  30–100 MB; 7.7 GB) scanned fresh: main, 2 workers: 116 s, **anon peak 2,706 MiB** of the 3,072 MiB limit. This branch,
  4 workers + budget: 132 s, 2,591 MiB. One worker: 213 s, 2,041 MiB.
- **One file alone is the problem.** Per-file anon peaks with one worker: Questoris_V2.3mf (176 MB) 2,024 MiB,
  Immortal.3mf (179 MB) 1,903, Knight_Lancer.3mf (174 MB) 1,830; a 193 MB STL about 950 MiB. So a 3MF peaks at about
  **11–12× its size** and an STL about **5×**, not the 6× / 2× the budget assumes.
- The whole corpus with this branch: 914 s (main 888 s) — no faster: the scan is CPU-bound at the container's 2 CPUs.
