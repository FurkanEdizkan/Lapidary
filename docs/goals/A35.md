# A35 — Grid pages land equally fast at card 10,000

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `perf/grid-pages`
**Wave:** 16 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A34 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Scale report items 3 and 7. Scrolling stays at 60 fps, but each new page takes longer to appear as more are loaded
(0.45 s at 1k cards, 1.35 s at 7.5k), while the server answers in 3–6 ms. And only drawn cards exist, so closing quick
look on a far card cannot hand focus back to it, and Tab leaves the grid at the last drawn row.

## Decisions

- Profile a page landing at 1k and 8k in headless Chrome first, and remove what grows.
- The focused card is always drawn; focus return scrolls it into view first.

## Done when

A page lands in about the same time at 1k and 10k cards; focus returns to a far card; a keyboard check in the rig; gate
and rig pass.

## Record

The check stack (19,194 parts), headless Chrome at 1440×900, each page made to land by jumping to the end:
- **The cause:** scrolled past the grid's last row, the window held no rows, so there was no card to measure, and the
  grid fell back to drawing *every* card loaded for one frame (a MutationObserver counted 3,000 cards removed and
  added on a page landing at 3,000). That was the `removeChild` and motion's `measureScroll` in the profile, and it
  grew with every page. The window now keeps its last rows, and a measurement with nothing to measure keeps the window
  it had. A jsdom test with the layout stubbed fails without it.
- **A page landing:** 210 ms median at 1k and 1,868 ms at 8k before; **61–87 ms median at every thousand up to 10k**
  now. Heap with 10,000 cards loaded: 412 MB → 48 MB; 84 cards in the DOM.
- **Keyboard:** Tab walks 150 cards without leaving the grid (it did before too: the overscan rows are drawn). Closing
  the stage after 150 arrow steps now puts focus on the card it stepped to, because the grid follows the part open in a
  look. And the narrow quick look gave focus back to whatever a click on the picture had focused, the grid around the
  card, rather than the card's name; it now returns to the name, as the stage did. New rig flow `keyboard`.
Rig 30/30, gate 14/14.
