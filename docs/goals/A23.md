# A23 — Part page: the stage and the filmstrip

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `feat/part-stage`
**Wave:** 13 · **Kind:** web · **Depends on:** A16, A21 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

The redesign (REDESIGN.md, owner 2026-10-18) and A17's audit: with no picture the stage is a 600px black void under
a full-width View bar and the pictures sit far below; quick look has no stage at all.

## Decisions

- Follow DESIGN.md § The stage and filmstrip. The main case is the part with no picture: the lamp, the still lattice,
  the format, and View on the stage as an overlay control (its name unchanged). With pictures: the whole first
  picture on the lamp (A16), and a filmstrip under the stage; choosing a picture crossfades the stage in 180 ms. A15's
  drag reorder and move buttons stay; the first is still the card's picture.
- Quick look shares the stage. Opening a part still reads no model file.

## Done when

The part page and quick look, with and without pictures, at 1440 and 390, match DESIGN.md; the rig's part-detail,
view-original, view-tools, pictures and delete flows and the gate pass.

## Record

**Built** (`web/src/components/PartDetail.tsx`, `QuickLook.tsx`, `lib/motion-react.tsx`, `lib/strings.ts`):
- **The stage** (`Preview`) is always a stage: the `stage-lamp` pool, 10px radius. No picture (the main case): `<Lattice
  still />` centred on the lamp, the format as a mono chip at the stage's foot, and **View in 3D** as an overlay control
  (Machined Slate plate, Edge, 7px) at the foot's right; no WebGL puts its sentence on the same plate. While the pictures
  load the lamp is empty, so a pictured part never flashes the lattice. With pictures: the chosen one whole (`contain`).
  The lattice or picture is the Viewer's poster, so pressing View never blanks the stage. Nothing fetches a model file
  until View is pressed (`mounts` test and the rig's quick-look / part-detail flows).
- **The filmstrip** (`Filmstrip`, replacing `Gallery`) sits 12px under the stage (was ~650px). 64px filled tiles, each a
  button named `images.alt(name, i)`; the current one has `aria-current` and a 2px Quicklime underline. Arrow keys and
  Home/End walk the strip and move the stage with the focus. Choosing crossfades over 180 ms (new `Crossfade` primitive
  in `motion-react.tsx`, `transition-none`, zero under reduced motion; `Presence initial={false}` so the first picture
  doesn't fade under `flipFrom`). Measured at ~80 ms: opacities 0.16 / 0.84; reduced motion: 1 at once. A15's drag, the
  move earlier/later buttons and "On the card" stay on the tiles; the add controls are the strip's last tile. Since a
  click on a tile now chooses it, the card framing (`Framed`: focal point and fit) is a row under the strip for the
  chosen picture, labelled "On a card", owner only. Read-only with at most one picture, there is no strip.
- **Quick look** renders the same stage (16rem) and filmstrip, then name/tools, geometry, the recorded matter, the rest.
  The pane fades in by presence (`Fade`, opacity only, so `flipFrom` still measures true); `panel-in` left it.
- **The column:** every section opens on a label over a Scribe Line. Tags, materials and sources open from a quiet **Add**
  in that row (names `tags.open` "Add a tag", `materials.open` "Add a material", `sources.add` now "Add a source", which
  passes label-in-name); Fetch details opens from its own quiet button with submit "Fetch" (`sources.fetchNow`). Forms
  close on Escape or Cancel and stay open after a save. Copy link is quiet beside Download. At lg the column's second
  row takes the stage's slack (`grid-rows-[auto_1fr]`), so Geometry sits right under Download.
- The stage column's `scrim-in` is gone. The keyframes stay in `styles.css`: Menu and Upload still use them.

**Measured:** entry `index-*.js` 85.74 kB gzip after rebasing on A20 and A24 (84.30 before this goal; budget 99.0). Gate
green (`target/verify-A23.log`). Rig 27/27, run twice, the second after rebasing. Screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844,
with and without reduced motion, in `/mnt/Storage2/lapidary-a23-shots`: the part page with no picture and with three,
the crossfade half way, the second picture, the View overlay, View open, and the quick look with and without a picture.

**Tests:** two new ones in `parts.$partId.test.tsx` (the strip puts the chosen picture on the stage, the arrows walk it
and move focus, the framing follows; a part with no picture has the lamp, the lattice and its format on the stage). The
tags and materials tests now press Add first. Every other accessible name is unchanged.

**Decided without the owner:** card framing moved off the thumbnail into one row under the strip; the format chip shows
over a picture as well; the order hint moved into the strip's `title`.

**Left for later:** the strip doesn't scroll sideways; a part with many pictures wraps onto a second row.
