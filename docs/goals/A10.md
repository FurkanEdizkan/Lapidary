# A10 — Upload single files, and zip and 7z archives

**Status:** merged
**Branch:** `feat/upload-archives`
**Wave:** 12 · **Kind:** Rust + web · **Depends on:** A9 · **Migrations:** —

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-18: "we couldn't upload the model 3mf and zip 7z etc files." The Upload button opened a folder picker
only, so a single `.3mf` could not be chosen, and an uploaded archive was never opened.

## Decisions

- Upload offers **files** and **a folder**, in the header and in the Add models dialog.
- An uploaded `.zip` or `.7z` is kept as it was uploaded, and its meshes (`stl`, `obj`, `3mf`) are unpacked beside it
  into `uploads/<user>/<archive name>/`, each indexed as its own part, in the upload's batch. A part's source path is
  `<archive path without its extension>/<path inside>`, so uploading the same pack again skips or revises.
- Unpacking never writes over a different file (the same bytes are reused; different ones get `name (2)`), never
  leaves the folder (zip `enclosed_name`, the escaping check for 7z), skips links and nested archives by name, and has
  limits per entry, in total and in entries. STEP and IGES inside are refused by name and not unpacked.
- RAR is refused by name: the only reader is non-free C++ (`unrar`). Encrypted archives and unsupported zip methods
  are refused by name. Archives found by a **scan** stay unread: unpacking them would write beside the owner's files.
- New dependency: `sevenz-rust2` (pure Rust, Apache-2.0); `zip` gains `deflate64`.

## Done when

A real `.3mf`, `.zip` and `.7z` from the corpus upload through the new picker and become parts; a `.rar` says why
not; the gate and the rig pass.

## Record

- Web: Upload opens a menu, **Files…** (`stl, obj, 3mf, zip, 7z`, and `rar`, STEP and IGES so they are refused by
  name rather than hidden) or **A folder…**; Add models offers Upload files and Upload a folder. The first-run
  button still uploads a folder.
- `archive.rs` (lapidary-ingest): `unpack` streams each entry through a capped reader into
  `InboxWriter::put_or_reuse` (lapidary-storage): the same bytes reused, different ones beside as `name (2)`, never
  over a file. Limits 2 GB an entry, 8 GB in all, 20,000 entries (`ponytail:`). zip names through `enclosed_name`, 7z
  through the escaping check; links, nested archives, `__MACOSX` and dot files left out. **A solid 7z is one stream:
  every entry not kept is read through**, or the next one fails its checksum — 24 of 25 real 7z packs did until it was.
- `handler.rs`: an upload's job (`ingest_upload`) opens a zip or 7z and queues one `IngestInbox` per model into its
  batch as `<archive without extension>/<path inside>`; a RAR is refused with a sentence; anything else is indexed as
  before. `refuse_b_rep` (api) counts an archive as something to take. `ARCHIVE_EXTENSIONS` in lapidary-core.
- A Lapidary bundle uploaded as a plain file unpacks its meshes as plain parts; Import a bundle is still the way to
  bring one in with its history.
- Checks: unit tests (a zip with a mesh, a 3MF, a STEP, junk, an escaping name and a nested archive; a retry; a folder
  already there; limits; broken archives; a solid 7z with a photo ahead of the model), `put_or_reuse`'s test, a handler
  test (a zip unpacked into its batch and indexed; a RAR refused), an api test (an archive beside a STEP is taken).
  Corpus: 50 real archives (25 zip, 25 7z) unpacked with none refused, and all 89 real 3MFs parsed. Rig: seed 17 ok,
  flows 26 ok, the new `upload-files` uploading a loose 3MF, a zip and a 7z through the real Files….
- **Left.** RAR (110 in the owner's corpus): the only reader is non-free C++. Archives found by a scan, and those a
  link import downloads, stay unread (P1). tar and tar.gz are not opened.
