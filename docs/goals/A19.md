## Why

## Decisions

- `Doorway` gains an `aside`: at `lg` a 55/45 grid, the lattice panel left on the lamp, the form right (`max-w-sm`);
  below `lg` a short lattice band above the form. Setup (A20) uses the same frame.
- The lattice is a pure, deterministic `lib/lattice.ts` (a low-poly part as points and unique edges inside a viewBox)
  drawn by `components/Lattice.tsx` through `drawIn` (anime.js, `lib/motion.ts`). It draws once, settles to grey in
  under 5 s, and is still after; the draw head is the accent only while it moves. Under reduced motion it is drawn.
- The sign-in form keeps its labels, names and behaviour. The lattice loads lazily, never on the grid's path.

## Done when

duplicate edges, everything inside the viewBox, deterministic) and the gate pass; the entry chunk stays within A18's
budget.

## Record

**Built.**
- `web/src/lib/lattice.ts`: a pure, deterministic 12-tooth spur gear with a raised hub and a bore, 348 unique edges
  projected at a fixed three-quarter view (spin 7°, tilt −48°, turn 20°, weak perspective) into `0 0 600 <h>`, the
  viewBox as tall as the part needs. Each edge carries `depth` (0 near, 1 far) and `inner` (the face triangulation).
  Draw order: front profile, axial corners, back profile, hub rings, bore, then the face fan and the hub's top.
- `web/src/lib/lattice.test.ts`: edge count 250–400, no duplicate edge either way, every point and depth in range,
  two calls equal.
- `web/src/components/Lattice.tsx`: settled strokes per DESIGN.md (outline in Edge, fainter with depth; mesh in the
  Scribe Line), 1px non-scaling. It draws through `drawIn` inside `--duration-intro` (900 ms, read from the token) with accent copies as the draw head, removed
  on `onDone`. `still` renders the final frame at once with no `drawIn` and shares one prebuilt path tree, for A21/A23's
  missing-picture stand-in (owner/lead, 2026-10-18; DESIGN.md § Lattice still says "nowhere else", which A21/A23 will
  need to amend). Under reduced motion every Lattice is still and the accent is never rendered.
- `web/src/components/Lattice.test.tsx`: aria-hidden, one path per edge, no accent stroke at rest; first-frame
  markup shows the head only on a lattice about to draw (mutation-checked: dropping `!still` fails it).
- `drawIn(lines, onDone, total, heads?)` in `motion.ts`: optional accent heads, one per line, drawn as the last 8 % of
  each line's tip and faded over its last `--duration-fast` through anime's `waapi`, on the same stagger, so each line
  is grey the moment it is complete. Existing callers unchanged.
- `Doorway` takes an optional `aside`: at `lg` a 55/45 grid (`min-h-dvh`), the aside on `stage-lamp` with the
  diamond, wordmark and `strings.doorway.pitch` ("A visual index for your 3D part library.") in Ash at its foot; the
  form `max-w-sm` centred. Below `lg` a 9rem band, the drawing larger than the band and cropped by it, to the right
  of the mark. Without an aside the old markup is returned verbatim (setup untouched).
- `login.tsx`: the aside holds a `lazy()` Lattice. Labels, names, fields, the flow and errors are unchanged; the
  forgot-password note moved under the button, which no longer wraps at 390.
