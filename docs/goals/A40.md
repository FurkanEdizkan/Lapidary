## Record

- **Blank grid after its count shrank** (an A35 regression): the window is worked out again from its last row height
  when no card is left to measure, and clamped at render. A jsdom test fails without it. On the 20k stack in Chrome,
  a cached search while scrolled deep draws its 48 cards.
- **One refresh per finished batch,** not one per page that lands after it (an index test fails without it: 3 first
  page fetches instead of 2).
- **Scroll observer:** built once per `hasMore`, reads `onMore` and `fetching` through a ref, skips while a page is in
  flight, and looks again when one lands.
- **Selection scope** includes the field filters.
- **Focus after a look:** the grid holds one target until the page holds its card (the following part, or the card a
  closed look hands focus to), then lets go. On the 20k stack: closing the pane after scrolling ten screens away puts
  focus on its card; a deep-linked part 46 gets focus back on close. A jsdom test covers a card 1,500 away.
- **The stage** asks for the next page at the last loaded part and steps on when it lands; its position reads
  "48 of 50+" while there is more.
- **Known, left:** a deep link opens the pane but the grid is not scrolled to the card while it is open (the page's
  own scroll to the top on load lands after it). Focus on close is right.
Rig 30/30 (`keyboard` now also closes the pane after scrolling away), gate 14/14.
